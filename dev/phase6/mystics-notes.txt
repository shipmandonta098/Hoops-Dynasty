WHAT TO WATCH FOR IN 2024


 Team
    • The Mystics are set to play 40 regular season games for the second-straight season. The 40-game sea-
      son is an increase from the WNBA’s 36-game season in 2022.
    • Washington has 10 afternoon games this season, with eight of the 10 games having a 3:00 p.m. tip-time
      and two games with a 2:00 p.m. tip time.
    • The Mystics will play their 900th regular season game on Sunday, September 8, 2024, vs. Minnesota.
    • The Mystics look to average the most assists and most made threes per game in franchise history after
      averaging a franchise high 22.1 assists and 9.8 made threes per game prior to the Olympic break. The
      current end-of-season highs are 21.9 assists per game (2019) and 9.3 made threes per game (2019).
    • Washington looks to average 80 or more points per game for the eighth time over the last nine seasons.
      The team averaged 80.5 points per game in 2023 and 79.3 points per game prior to the Olympic break.
    • The Mystics will look to dish out 20+ assists per game for the third time in franchise history (2022, 2019).
