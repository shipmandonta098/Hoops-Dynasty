Hoops Dynasty — Phase 12: WNBA port and final-minute pace
21 October 2026

Production update: WNBA transition/roster port, WNBA hold+quick, and shorter final-minute lead-protection setup. Statistical gaps remain; this is not a claim of complete league calibration. Scope: modern (2020 onward) leagues. Accepted Phase 11 baseline retained. No accuracy boosts, forced ties, outcome caps or save-schema changes. Roster allocation applies only when creating new fictional teams; existing rosters and completed results are preserved.

3. Hold+quick: OT and tied-start outcomes only

Correction to Phase 11: full-season margin/blowout differences across diverging careers were not valid grounds to reject a tied-game policy. No margin or blowout gate is used here. Hold+quick includes a hold rule when tied under 24 seconds AND down-two shot preference within 30 seconds; the latter can act after a tied start becomes a deficit. Synthetic cases use production possession/shot/foul/rebound resolvers with injected starting scores. Tied 60 and tied 30 runs continue through actual OT; tied 20 captures the regulation endpoint, with a tie defining OT entry under the unchanged engine rule. Two hundred matched samples verify exact endpoint/full-resolver regulation equality. Actual overtime is also played in all full-game samples.

4. Final-minute timing and overtime recheck

Shorter setup candidate: final 60 seconds, leading by 1–6, no transition, modern league: setup allowance 4 seconds instead of the Phase 7 protection allowance up to 15. Other resolver time still elapses normally. Intentional-foul probability and its two-second delay are unchanged. The 200-game 8-second pilot is retained separately (world 712), not pooled with fresh world 714 evaluation.

Real method:Q4 final 60, fouling team down 3–6; preceding made FG or final made ordinary FT, followed by non-shooting personal/take foul without intervening live-ball action. Elapsed game clock from score to foul proxies legal inbound touch-to-foul. PBP does not explicitly annotate intentionality or inbound catch; no direct tracking measurement is claimed. Sim subset also requires preceding opponent completed possession ending with a score at the next possession start. Larger all-possession-start-to-intentional-foul samples are preserved separately. Tiny real samples and selection limitations prevent precise delay calibration. The sim two-second delay is near the real median, so it was not shortened.

6. Validation, scope and remaining gaps

Roster identity/attribute/position-count preservation checks passed for NBA/WNBA 1997,2006,2024. Pre-2020 initial rosters and modern NBA initial rosters are unchanged by the port. Timing scope constraints passed. Saved completed scores, box scores and summary events remain unchanged on load. Every full-game harness checks score/box identity, legal winner, and minute totals. Five full-season runs check complete 240-game schedules.
Remaining gaps: transition fast-break definition mismatch and residual excess; simulated team-margin spread may remain low; single-universe reset samples do not establish a league-wide OT rate; no historical/pre2020 statistical recalibration. No global clock multiplier or shooting-accuracy change was used.
