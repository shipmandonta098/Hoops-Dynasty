DEFINITIONS AND LIMITS
Mean margin = absolute final score difference, including all OT. 20+ uses >=20; within5 uses <=5. OT = at least one overtime period. Team-margin SD = population standard deviation across teams of their signed season mean margins, computed per season then averaged.
Transition scorecard = primary FGA carrying the simulation fast-break opportunity flag divided by ALL box-score FGA, including untagged putbacks/kickouts. Real denominator = every official PBP isFieldGoal attempt, tagged numerator = qualifiers contains fastbreak, including misses. Opportunity share is a separate diagnostic.
Final60 = possession starts at 0<regulation time remaining<=60, summed across both teams, conditional on absolute margin<=6 at the 60-second checkpoint. Does not count a possession that began before the window. Real possession boundaries are reconstructed from control changes, made baskets, final made ordinary FTs, defensive rebounds and turnovers; offensive rebounds retain control. PBP is not direct inbound tracking, and inference/retained-ball edge cases can differ from the simulator.
Real full-season ranges are observed season ranges, not confidence intervals or universal pass/fail bands. PBP samples are retained convenience samples, not league censuses. Seed-cluster bootstrap uses 4,000 resamples of the ten worlds; it reflects this generated-world sample and cannot certify unseen worlds or other eras. WNBA simulated rules/size are 2024 (12 teams,40 games); 2025 real reference has13 teams,44 games.
The extra five calibration seasons were an explicitly recorded pre-freeze extension, not a second holdout; only the reserved 20 seasons are the final holdout. Calibration uses paired initial seeds, but changes can alter random-number consumption and later season paths. Aggregate differences are not isolated same-possession causal effects. Player identities, attributes, league-wide talent and position counts are preserved; roster swaps apply only to newly generated fictional modern WNBA careers. Existing saves, NBA allocation and pre-2020 generation are unchanged.
