# Franchise presets

Twelve optional fictional franchises. None is added to any league automatically, and existing careers are never changed without a user action. Once created, a preset team is an ordinary team: every field is editable and the save keeps no link back to the preset.

Geography is city-level reference data. The elevations for Calgary, Winnipeg, Ottawa, Anaheim and Green Bay come from city infoboxes, cross-checked by web search during implementation; Calgary sources range from 1,042 to 1,048 m. The other seven cities reuse the elevations already in `ALTITUDE_CITIES`. The coordinates for those seven are standard city-centre values that were not re-fetched, because the reference sites were not reachable from the build environment. Calgary rates *Moderate* altitude exposure for sea-level visitors (Denver is *High*). Shared nicknames such as Cincinnati Royals and Sacramento Royals carry no historical link; historical leagues never match teams by name.

## Where presets appear

- **New career → Add team → Franchise presets.** Logo, colors, arena, capacity and geography card. Use Edit team before starting to customize the city, name, abbreviation, colors, logo, arena or capacity.
- **Front office → Expansion → Start from a franchise preset.** Fills the expansion form (city, name, abbreviation, colors, arena, capacity, market, suggested division). Every field stays editable, and the preset logo and tertiary color stay with the club unless you clear the preset. The roster, expansion draft, cap and draft rules are the existing expansion system. Presets already in the league are hidden.
- **Team management → Relocate or rebrand from a preset.** Fills the editor; nothing changes until you save. The team keeps its id and key, roster, contracts, finances, records and franchise history. Archived box scores follow the new abbreviation. Altitude, city photo and rivalry geography follow the new city automatically.
- **Logo pickers.** In setup and team management, the default primary and secondary logo lists include the 12 preset logos after the 30 originals. Saved indexes 0–29 keep their meaning.

## Assets

- `dist/preset-logos/<ABBR>.svg` and `<ABBR>-secondary.svg`: original vector artwork (primary badge and secondary mark), generated by `dev/franchise-presets/build-logos.py`.
- `dist/preset-logos.png`: a 4×3 atlas (2048×1536, transparent), shown through `.franchise-logo.logo-preset`.
- `dist/secondary-logos/<ABBR>.png`: 512 px secondary marks used in standings, schedule, news, playoffs, draft and awards.
- Rebuild: `python3 dev/franchise-presets/build-logos.py && node dev/franchise-presets/render-logos.cjs`.

City photographs: none of the 12 cities has a photo in `dist/city-photos/`. All 12 slugs are registered (`anaheim`, `buffalo`, `calgary`, `cincinnati`, `green-bay`, `nashville`, `newark`, `ottawa`, `san-jose`, `st-louis`, `tampa`, `winnipeg`), so dropping an authorized `<slug>.jpg` into that folder enables it. Until then the dashboard shows the existing `dashboard-skyline.png` fallback. No era-specific images exist for these cities, and no historical photos were fabricated.

## Rivalries

Geography labels now distinguish *Cross-Town Feud* (same city), *Metro Rivalry* (Anaheim–Los Angeles, Newark–New York, San Jose–San Francisco/Oakland), *In-State* and *In-Province* rivalries. All of these keep the existing 50/50-crowd effect. A new *Regional Rivalry* (Buffalo–Toronto; Calgary, Winnipeg, Vancouver, Edmonton) adds heat but no crowd split. Distant pairs are never forced. See `RIVALRIES.md`.

## Not modelled

The engine has no geographic travel-distance or time-zone fatigue model. Coordinates and time zones are stored and displayed but do not affect simulation. Altitude does (existing system).

## Verification

- `node test-franchise-presets.cjs`: identities, uniqueness, untouched defaults, geography, altitude classes, logo markup and files, CSS, city-photo resolution, setup and save validation, expansion config, relocation continuity, rivalry classification.
- `node dev/franchise-presets/season-check.cjs nba|wnba`: full seasons (regular season, All-Star, playoffs, draft, free agency, rollover) with headless integrity validators, a preset expansion attempt, a relocation, the Day-3 debut rule, and save/load round trips.
- `node dev/franchise-presets/ui-check.cjs <dir>`: Chromium walkthrough with screenshots.

Results (seed 7101): NBA, a 32-team custom 82-game league with Calgary and Newark, plus Ottawa by relocation, passed 18 of 18 checks over two full seasons. WNBA, 12 teams with Tampa and Winnipeg, plus San Jose by relocation, also passed 18 of 18. The unit test runs a full preset expansion draft (Tampa: logo, arena, 18,200 seats, colors, guaranteed #4 pick, save/load).

## Known limitations (pre-existing, reproduced without presets)

1. **Round-robin calendars cannot be generated.** `scheduleOpeningNight` now throws "Cannot fit the featured opener…" whenever displaced day-1 games have no free date. Round-robin rounds have no free dates, so any NBA league that is not exactly 30 aligned teams fails to start when it uses the default 2-meeting format. Every expansion rebuild (always round-robin) also fails, which is why `test-expansion.cjs` stops at `newSeason()`. The same failure reproduces with the original quick presets (Las Vegas, Vancouver) and with plain team counts from 31 to 36. The older version (`dev/fixtures/opening-night-b0a5718.js`) fell back to `constrained:true` instead. Workaround: League settings → Custom season format. A 32-team custom 82-game league schedules and simulates correctly.
2. **The expansion draft rarely has enough players at the lottery window.** After a simulated season, CPU rosters sit at or near the minimum (NBA 14–15 of 14 minimum, WNBA 11 of 11), so the protection rules cannot supply a roster. The game shows its own "cannot supply" message.
3. **NBA lottery weights cover 14 lottery teams.** Leagues larger than 30 need another draft type, for example Reverse standings.
4. **The original quick presets** (Las Vegas, Vancouver, Montreal, Kansas City, Louisville, Pittsburgh, Baltimore, Mexico City) still borrow logos from the first eight default teams. They are unchanged here.
